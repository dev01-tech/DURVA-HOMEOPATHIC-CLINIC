About the Doctor

Dr. Jagruti A. Patel, B.H.M.S.

Dr. Jagruti A. Patel is a qualified homeopathic physician holding a Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (B.H.M.S.) degree from the esteemed Swaminarayan College. With over 2 years of clinical experience, Dr. Patel is dedicated to providing high-quality, professional homeopathic consultations with a focus on holistic recovery.

At Durva Homeopathic Clinic, the core philosophy centers on understanding each patient's unique health concerns. Dr. Patel believes that deep healing begins with listening carefully. By taking a comprehensive view of an individual's physical, mental, and lifestyle history, she provides personalized homeopathic consultations tailored specifically to their needs.

Our goal is to support your body's natural balance and guide you toward sustainable health. We respect your choices and make clinical support accessible through dedicated in-clinic sessions at our Vastral Road location in Ahmedabad, as well as online consultation options for patient comfort and accessibility.
